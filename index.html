Serving Summer Since 1996

Aggie’s Ice Cream in South Berwick, Maine, is a cherished destination for ice cream lovers seeking a delightful array of flavors. This popular spot offers an impressive selection that includes unique options like Milky Way and Blueberry Pie, ensuring there’s something for everyone. Customers rave about the rich creaminess and sweetness of every scoop, making it hard to choose just one flavor. The menu features various portion sizes, from the tiny pinky cone to larger scoops ideal for sharing.

For those looking for something special, Aggie’s also serves inventive sundaes like sundae spaghetti dinner with chocolate “meatballs,” adding a playful twist to traditional treats. With generous servings at great prices and friendly staff ready to help navigate through the extensive list of flavors, it’s no wonder this shop has become a family favorite over the years.
